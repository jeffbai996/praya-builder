## BCPL identity reference

The BCPL mark is mounted high on the right portion of the foreground library's glazed facade. Visually, it is a horizontal block-built composition with orange at the left, dark charcoal/black through the middle, a pale white section toward the right, and red at the right edge. Its offset, stepped silhouette and the arrangement of the colors matter; do not replace it with plain letters, an arbitrary book icon, or a newly invented emblem.

- The library has a strong horizontal roof edge, tall recessed glazing, closely spaced warm vertical members, dark framing and pale vertical accents.
- The logo sits on the glazing at a scale readable from the public approach; the entrance has its own smaller signs and lighting.
- Trees, hedges, stepped planted ground, paved walks and small lights shape the foreground and connect the building to changing terrain.
