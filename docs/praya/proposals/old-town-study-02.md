User-directed successor to the rejected R0: reference-led Praya architecture rather than transcription of the generated concept image.

- Adds all-elevation masonry framing, a detailed west lane, accessible planted east loggias, covered rear service court and accessible common roof garden.

- No invented address or institutional branding. Written signage and custom heads remain unsupported in this artifact.
