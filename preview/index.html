    WORKSPACE GUIDE

    Review a proposal

    Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, and scroll to zoom. Focus the model for arrow-key camera movement, +/- zoom, and R to reset.

    Use drawing layers for floor cutaways. Click a block to inspect its owner, then Focus component for a close view. Fit model frames the visible geometry. Expand view gives the model the screen; Escape returns to the workspace.

    Press / to search the register. Light, dark and OLED black themes and reviewer notes are stored in this browser. The register filters by building use and offers card and compact record views. Export schematic downloads the full revision for WorldEdit. Export review saves a portable JSON record; Save view downloads the current model as a PNG.
