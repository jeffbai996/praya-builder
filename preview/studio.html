          STUDIO
          Look, adjust, keep
          Orbit rotates around the building. Pan moves the view sideways or up and down. Free camera lets you drag to look, use WASD to move, Q/E to descend or rise, and Shift to move faster. Its buttons also work on touchscreens. Escape returns to Orbit; Fit view resets the framing. In Orbit, keys 1–6 pick a camera, F fits the building, E expands the model, R resets the view.
          Floor cutaway shows one level at a time. Adjust previews a material or roof change; Undo and Redo step through previews. Save version keeps the exact building; Compare shows what changed against any saved version. Request a revision downloads a file for an external design agent.
          Site holds surveys and new proposals. Build shows the connected server, prepares a copy for a matching site, and previews a saved version before construction.
