        STUDIO

        Look, adjust, keep

        Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Keys 1–6 pick a camera, F fits the building, E expands the model, R resets the view.

        Floor cutaway shows one level at a time. Adjust previews a material or roof change; Undo and Redo step through previews. Save version keeps the exact building; Compare shows what changed against any saved version. Request a revision downloads a file for an external design agent.

        Site holds surveys and new proposals. Construction previews and places a saved version through the isolated bridge only.
